## 🐛 Known Issues & Solutions

### **Issue: Portal Not Visible**
**Solution**: Portal is positioned at `[0, 0, -6]` in 3D space. Adjust camera FOV or position if needed.

### **Issue: Low FPS on Mobile**
**Solution**: Particle counts auto-adjust based on device. Further reduce star count in `SpaceBackground.js` if needed.

### **Issue: No Sound**
**Solution**: Sounds require user interaction first (browser policy). Click sound toggle after page loads.

### **Issue: Glitchy Animations**
**Solution**: Clear browser cache and ensure GPU acceleration is enabled.
